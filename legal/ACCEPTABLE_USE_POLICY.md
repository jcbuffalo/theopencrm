# Acceptable Use Policy — The Open CRM

**Effective date:** 2026-05-12
**Status:** DRAFT — pending counsel review (see [`LEGAL_TODO.md`](./LEGAL_TODO.md))

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to anyone who accesses or
uses The Open CRM (the "Service"). It is part of our
[Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md). Violations may result in
suspension or termination of your account.

## You may not

### Use the Service for illegal or harmful purposes

- Violate any law, regulation, or third-party right.
- Engage in fraud, including misrepresentation of identity, fake
  business communications, or fraudulent invoicing.
- Distribute malware, ransomware, spyware, or other malicious code.
- Engage in harassment, threats, hate speech, or content that incites
  violence.
- Distribute child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We will report any
  detection to NCMEC and the relevant authorities and cooperate fully
  with law enforcement.

### Send unwanted communications

- Send spam, unsolicited bulk email, or any communication that violates
  the U.S. CAN-SPAM Act, the European GDPR, or any other applicable
  anti-spam law.
- Use the Service to harvest email addresses or other contact
  information for spam purposes.
- Send messages that are designed to deceive (phishing, spoofing,
  social-engineering).

### Compromise security

- Attempt to access accounts, data, or systems that don't belong to
  you.
- Bypass authentication, rate limits, feature flags, or other access
  controls.
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service or its
  underlying infrastructure without our written authorization (good-
  faith security research subject to a coordinated-disclosure
  process is exempt — see "Security research" below).
- Use the Service to attack any third party (DDoS, port scanning,
  credential stuffing).

### Misuse the platform

- Resell access to the Service without a separate written agreement
  with us.
- Reverse-engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract source code from
  any non-open-source component of the Service.
- Use automation to create accounts in bulk, generate fake reviews, or
  artificially inflate any metric.
- Use the Service to host, distribute, or process content that
  infringes copyright, trademark, or other intellectual-property
  rights (see [DMCA Policy](./DMCA_POLICY.md) for the takedown
  procedure).
- Exceed reasonable use limits in a way that degrades performance for
  other users.

### Misrepresent your relationship with us

- Impersonate us, our employees, or our agents.
- Falsely claim endorsement, partnership, or affiliation.
- Use our trademarks, logos, or branding without written permission.

## Special categories

### Sensitive data

The Service is not specifically designed or certified for:

- **Protected Health Information (PHI)** subject to HIPAA. You may not
  use the Service to store or transmit PHI unless you have a signed
  Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with us. We do not currently
  offer one.
- **Children's data** subject to COPPA (data on users under 13). You
  may not collect, store, or process children's data through the
  Service.
- **Government-classified data** or any data subject to export
  controls (ITAR, EAR).
- **Payment card data** in scope of PCI-DSS. The Service does not
  process card data directly; we integrate with third-party payment
  processors for that.

If you need to process any of the above, contact us first to determine
whether the Service is appropriate.

### Security research

We welcome good-faith security research subject to a coordinated
disclosure process:

- Do not access or modify data you don't own.
- Do not degrade the Service or other users.
- Report findings privately to johncolesassistant@gmail.com before any
  public disclosure.
- Give us reasonable time (at least 90 days) to remediate before
  publishing.

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow this
process.

## Enforcement

We may, at our discretion:

- Issue a warning.
- Suspend your account pending investigation.
- Terminate your account without refund.
- Refer the matter to law enforcement.

We will use proportional enforcement — minor first-time violations
typically get a warning; egregious or repeated violations get
termination. Violations involving CSAM, fraud, or attacks on others
are grounds for immediate termination without warning.

## Reporting violations

If you believe someone is violating this AUP, contact us at
johncolesassistant@gmail.com with as much detail as you can share. We
investigate every credible report.

## Changes

We may update this AUP from time to time. Material changes will be
noticed via the in-app banner or email at least 30 days before they
take effect.
